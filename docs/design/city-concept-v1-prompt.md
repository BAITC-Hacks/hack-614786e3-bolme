## Промпт

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one highly polished 16:9 desktop game-interface concept for a Russian-language voice-controlled city management simulator set in an imagined compact Astana. It should have the grounded realistic materials and atmosphere of a premium city-builder such as Cities: Skylines, while being a small achievable district scene with around 60-100 architecturally coherent buildings, not an enormous metropolis, not a toy floating island, not flat low-poly boxes. Camera is a cinematic elevated three-quarter perspective looking over a river and a bridge, mid-rise courtyard apartment blocks, several restrained glass high-rises, parks, road markings, a few buses and cars, detailed sidewalks and believable deciduous trees. Realistic scale, layered beige and off-white facade modules, visible window grids and balconies, roof details. Warm late-afternoon sun, long soft directional shadows, subdued blue water, gentle atmospheric perspective, crisp readable scene, subtle filmic color grading, restrained contact shadows. The city fills about 80 percent of screen and continues naturally toward a softly hazy horizon. Five subtle district outlines, only one selected district outlined in fine teal. In the selected district, a proposed modern low-rise clinic appears as a semi-transparent turquoise ghost building on an empty plot next to existing apartments. This ghost object must be plainly distinct from the permanent buildings. The user is previewing an action before confirmation. Slim elegant dark translucent UI, understated typography, no clutter and no sci-fi holographic console. Top-left small title 'АКИМ' and small caption 'КОНЦЕПТ'. Top center three clear compact counters: 'Бюджет 100', 'Решения 0/5', 'Score 52.56'. On the map, one small district label 'НУРА'. At right a beautiful compact dark translucent proposal card that occupies no more than 20% of the screen: heading 'Поликлиника', district 'Нура', text 'Стоимость: 20', text 'Медпомощь: 35 → 43.75', a teal button 'Принять' and a quiet secondary button 'Отмена'. Bottom center a small integrated voice interaction bar, a simple microphone icon, a short elegant live waveform, and the text 'Обсудить решение'. This is a design concept illustration, not evidence of a working app. No browser chrome, no device frame, no watermarks, no extraneous labels or logos. Focus on exceptional architecture, lighting, composition, and a clear relation between voice, pending proposal, and city.
